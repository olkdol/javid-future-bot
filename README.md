## What it is

JaviD Future Bot is a desktop tool that runs three AI-researched quant strategies on
Binance USDⓈ-M futures, scanning the market every 10 seconds. It is a **tool**, not a
"guaranteed profit" service, and not a promise of returns.

## Key features

- **Non-custodial by design** — your API keys never leave your PC, withdrawal permission is
  never required, and the bot **cannot** move or withdraw your funds. You grant only
  *Reading*, *Spot & Margin*, and *Futures* permissions, plus IP restriction.
- **Three quant strategies** — Strategy‑A (trend), Strategy‑B (mean reversion),
  Strategy‑C (breakout), each AI‑researched and back‑tested before any rule was locked in.
- **Risk‑free Shadow Mode** — paper‑trade on real, live Binance market data with virtual
  funds. Zero real money at risk until you decide to go live.
- **Telegram reports** — balance, P&L, and win‑rate updates sent to your phone.
- **Free** — no price tag and no subscription. It stays free to run when you create your
  Binance account through the referral link on the website.

## How it works

```
Your PC  ⇄  Binance API        (no server in the middle — not even ours)
   │
   ├─ 3 strategies scan every 10s
   ├─ enter / exit automatically on rules
   └─ Shadow Mode: live data, virtual funds, zero real risk
```
